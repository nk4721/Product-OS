# Vanthom Intelligence — Positioning and Messaging

## Status

Working hypotheses for discovery. These messages are not approved market claims until customer evidence and product capability support them.

## Positioning hypothesis

**Category:** AI-powered decision support  
**Primary audience:** Product, commercial and operational leaders who make recurring decisions using fragmented information  
**Alternative:** Manual research, spreadsheets, document search, meetings, generic AI chat and bespoke analysis  
**Distinctive promise:** Traceable recommendations that separate evidence, assumptions and judgement  
**Reason to believe:** Governed source access, specialist analysis workflows, human approval and an auditable decision history

## Recommended message hierarchy

1. Start with the decision the customer needs to make.
2. Show how approved information becomes a traceable decision brief.
3. Explain uncertainty, controls and human accountability.
4. End with the measurable customer outcome.
5. Avoid broad claims about autonomous intelligence.

### Short sales narrative

Ni har sannolikt redan mycket av informationen som krävs för att fatta bättre beslut. Problemet är att den finns på flera platser, tolkas olika och tar tid att sammanställa.

Vanthom Intelligence börjar med beslutet ni behöver fatta. Tjänsten använder endast godkända källor, skiljer fakta från antaganden och visar hur rekommendationen har tagits fram. Resultatet är ett tydligt beslutsunderlag som en ansvarig person kan granska, justera och omsätta i handling.

Värdet ska mätas i bättre utfall: kortare förberedelsetid, tydligare ansvar, färre omtag och bättre spårbarhet.

## Benefits to validate

Do not publish these as quantified claims until measured:

- reduce preparation time for recurring decisions
- improve consistency across teams
- make assumptions and gaps visible earlier
- reduce rework caused by missing context
- preserve decisions and learning for reuse
- strengthen trust through source traceability

## Objections to test

| Objection | Responsible response |
|---|---|
| “We cannot expose our data to AI.” | Start with approved sources, explicit permissions, purpose limitation and documented retention. Validate the actual required controls. |
| “A generic AI assistant can already do this.” | Test whether governed context, repeatable workflows, traceability and decision history create meaningful additional value. |
| “AI recommendations cannot be trusted.” | Show evidence, uncertainty and conflicts; require accountable human approval. Never claim certainty that is not supported. |
| “Every decision is different.” | Test a narrow recurring workflow before claiming a reusable product. |
| “We already have dashboards.” | Position dashboards as inputs; validate whether the unresolved need is interpretation, synthesis and action. |

## Claims policy

Marketing must:

- distinguish validated capability from roadmap
- avoid claiming that AI replaces accountable decision-makers
- avoid implying access to data outside customer permissions
- preserve the identity and restrictions of supplier data
- use customer outcomes only with documented evidence
- explain that generated recommendations require review
